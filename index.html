About Me

I bring design and engineering together to make complex products clearer.

I started my career in engineering, building full-stack products and working closely with the people who used them. That closeness to real usage is what shaped how I design now.

Today, I use both sides of that experience, technical and design, to think through interaction, information architecture, and the realities of implementation, from the first structure in Figma to a product that actually ships.

The work I care about most is rarely about making an interface look good. It's about staying close enough to a product to see where people get stuck, and building the judgment to know which of those moments are actually worth fixing.
